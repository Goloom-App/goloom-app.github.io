Content calendar

The content calendar is the planning surface for everything your team has scheduled. It shows posts across every connected account in one place.

Views

Section titled “Views”
  • A grid calendar view for the month/week at a glance.
  • Each entry shows its target accounts and scheduled time.

Rescheduling

Section titled “Rescheduling”

Move a post to a new slot to reschedule it. Times are timezone-aware, so what you plan is what publishes.

Cancelling

Section titled “Cancelling”

Cancel a scheduled post from the calendar before it publishes. Over the API, posts also expose a cancel action under /v1/teams/{teamID}/posts.

Working ahead

Section titled “Working ahead”

Combine the calendar with automation (recurring templates and RSS rules) to keep channels active without planning every post by hand.
